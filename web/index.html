Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It runs on free tiers end to end. The only limit is 100 tracked items per account.

How often are prices checked?

Every item is re-checked about every 6 hours. New items and "check now" requests run on the next pass, within about 15 minutes.

Why Telegram?

It's free, instant, and doesn't need your phone number. You connect once from inside the app; alerts go to your own chat.

What if a store blocks bots?

The tracker falls back to a real browser, and if a store still says no you'll see a plain-language note on the item instead of a cryptic error. It keeps trying.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app, and you can share a product link straight to it from the store's app or browser.

Can I get my data out, or delete it?

Both, from the account menu: export as CSV or JSON, or delete every item, price and alert along with your Telegram link.
